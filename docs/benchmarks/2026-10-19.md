The comparison is this library's source generator against .NET's: each Industry workload as a `[GeneratedRegex]` member on both sides, `Count` over the text. The Corvus rows carry the `u` flag (the benchmark adds `gu` to every row, for the interpreter rows too), which .NET has no mode for: classes decode surrogate pairs and the `i` flag folds `s` to the long s and `k` to the Kelvin sign. Counts agree on 53 of the 57 rows; the four apart are `$` and `.` at CRLF, where ECMA-262 counts CR as a line terminator, and `\w`, which .NET takes as Unicode letters and digits. Two rows the row parser drops are absent from the table below.
