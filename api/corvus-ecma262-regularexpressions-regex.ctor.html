Regex(string, RegexFlags, TimeSpan)

Source: Regex.cs

Initializes a new instance of the Regex class with a match timeout: the time each match may take before it is abandoned with RegexMatchTimeoutException. A match with a timeout is also abandoned, with ExceededMemory, when its backtracking stack reaches its cap. Every member that matches gives each of its matches the timeout, whatever the pattern. Without one a match has no limit of time or of memory, so a pattern or a text from outside should be given one, here or for every instance with DefaultMatchTimeout.

public Regex(string pattern, RegexFlags flags, TimeSpan matchTimeout)
