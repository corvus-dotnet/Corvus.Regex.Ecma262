Regex

Definition

Namespace: Corvus.Ecma262.RegularExpressions
Assembly: Corvus.Regex.Ecma262.dll
Source: Regex.cs

An ECMA-262 regular expression: the grammar and the answers of ECMAScript 2025, with the shape of System.Text.RegularExpressions.Regex. It matches UTF-16 text (String and ReadOnlySpan of Char) and UTF-8 text (ReadOnlySpan of Byte) natively, with no transcoding either way. An instance is immutable and safe to share between threads.
